CASE STUDY 01 · MONTHLY BOOKKEEPING

Maple Leaf Landscaping — June 2026 Books

ExcelChart of AccountsBank ReconciliationFinancial Statements

The Problem

A small landscaping business has a month of unedited bank transactions with cryptic descriptions and no categories, so the owner can't see income, expenses or profit.

What I Did

Set up a chart of accounts, coded every transaction, reconciled the books to the bank statement, then built the Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet from the ledger.

The Outcome

Books agree with the bank to the cent, the Balance Sheet balances, and the owner sees a clear picture of profit for the month.

CASE STUDY 02 · ACCOUNTS PAYABLE

AP Payment Monitoring & Expense Tracking

ExcelQuickBooks OnlineInvoice VerificationPayment Reconciliation

The Problem

Vendor invoices arrive with different due dates and document gaps. Without one tracker, it's hard to know what's paid, what's pending, and what shouldn't be paid yet.

What I Did

Logged each invoice with a unique reference, checked payee, amount and supporting documents, tracked payment method and bank clearing, and categorized every expense.

The Outcome

Invoices missing a PO or with mismatched totals were held before payment, and open items were visible at a glance before reporting.

STEP 2Verify payee, amount, due date, documents
